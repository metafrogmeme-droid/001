What it does

The short list. What ships, and what does not, is on the product page.

  • Talk to it. The chat reads your portfolio, your risk state, the macro calendar and a live scan through read-only tools, gated by the same permission table as the commands — it cannot reach anything a typed sentence could not.

  • Answers stream as they are written, on the web and in Telegram, and every streamed fragment is replaced by the checked final text.

  • Every engine call and every Arena trade is hashed at decision time. Daily Merkle roots and per-call receipts you can re-derive in your own browser.

  • Paper first. A virtual account with live prices and liquidation mechanics, competition seasons, and a public board that publishes percentages and counts, never account balances.

  • Bring your own keys. Exchange credentials are validated read-only and encrypted at rest, and live trading is switched on per user by the operator — never by default.

  • Guardian: Flight Recorder, Stress Lab, Risk Sentinel, Transaction Firewall, Escape Agent and Intent Compiler. They warn, simulate and prove; none of them moves funds.

  • Built for other agents too: an MCP server, public read-only endpoints, an ERC-8257 tool manifest and an ERC-8004 identity.

  • Fourteen interface languages, and a chat that answers in thirty-four.

An anchor stores a 32-byte hash, the publisher who submitted it, and a label the publisher chooses. The hash is a commitment to a file’s bytes: hash the same file and you can see whether those exact bytes were submitted, and by whom. The contract does not keep the file. A matching hash is not proof that what the file says is true, and it is not a trading result.

This is separate from the call hashes further up the page. Those are RUNECLAW’s own record. This website does not submit a hash, and it does not sign or send a transaction.

No file hash had been committed when this page was written

On 6 November 2026 the contract log on the explorer contained the deployment events only. There was no Anchored event. This sentence is that reading, not a live counter. Anything anchored later is on the contract page, not here.
