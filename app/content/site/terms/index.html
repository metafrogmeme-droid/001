Last updated 17 October 2026

These terms cover RUNECLAW, run by Humanoid Traders (“we”): the website at www.humanoid-traders.com, the Telegram bot, and the paper Arena as an app in the Pi Browser and in Farcaster. Using any of them means you accept these terms. What we collect, and what you can delete, is on the privacy page.

Not financial advice

Signals, scores, confidence figures, charts and the agent’s replies are produced by software from market data. They can be wrong, late or incomplete. None of them is investment advice or a recommendation to buy or sell anything. You make your own decisions and you carry their results.

The Arena is paper trading

  • Every Arena account starts with the same 10,000 vUSDT virtual balance and trades it at live market prices.
  • vUSDT is a number on our server, not a currency or a token. It cannot be bought, sold, withdrawn or exchanged for anything, and it has no cash value.
  • A place on a season board measures paper results. Paper results do not predict what the same trades would do with real money.

Real trading, if you connect an exchange

Where the product offers live trading, orders go to your own exchange account with your own API keys, and every order and its result is yours. Leveraged trading can lose your entire balance. The risk checks reduce some risks and remove none: software, exchanges and networks fail. Use exchange keys scoped to trading, never to withdrawal.

Tips in the Pi app

  • A tip is optional. It unlocks nothing, buys no service, and changes nothing about your account or the Arena.
  • The amounts are the ones the app offers, and you confirm the payment in Pi’s own payment screen.
  • A completed tip is a transfer on the Pi blockchain. We cannot reverse it, and there is no refund.
  • Pi Network is not a party to these terms, and does not sponsor or endorse RUNECLAW.
