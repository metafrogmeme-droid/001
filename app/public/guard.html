🛡️ Claw Guard

Check a transaction or a signature before you sign it. Claw Guard says in plain words what it grants and, for a transaction, what it would do to your account on Base right now. It never signs, sends or holds a key.

What leaves this page: your request goes to RUNECLAW’s server, which decodes it and, for a transaction with your address, asks Base’s public endpoints to simulate it. Nothing is stored unless you are signed in; then only a hash of the request and the verdict are sealed.

A verdict is a reading with reasons, not a guarantee. Unknown is not the same as safe. Check the address, the amount and the approval scope yourself. A safety aid, not investment advice.
