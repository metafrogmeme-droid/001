How it works

Three steps, and you can verify the second one yourself.

  1. 1

    The engine reads the market

    It scans every Bitget USDT-M perp for momentum, funding, open interest and structure, then forms a call: symbol, direction, entry, stop, target, confidence and the pattern it read.

  2. 2

    The call is sealed

    RUNECLAW hashes each call the moment it is made — symbol, direction, entry, stop, target, confidence and the pattern it read. The outcome attaches to that sealed record afterwards and cannot alter it. No backdated wins, no quietly deleted losers. Paper-trade free in your browser; go live only on your own exchange keys.

Why RUNECLAW is different

Most bots ask you to trust a black box. RUNECLAW is built to earn it.

Explainable, not a black box

Every decision is sealed into a tamper-evident ledger you can re-inspect — inputs, reasoning, the risk verdict and the outcome. Read the decision, don't just trust the P&L.

Guarded, not just optimized

A whole safety layer around every trade — a digital-twin stress lab, a systemic-risk radar, a transaction firewall and an escape planner — not just a chase for returns.

Non-custodial by design

Verifiable, not claimed
