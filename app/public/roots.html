🌳 Daily Seal Roots

Every trading call and Arena paper trade is sealed the moment it's made. At the end of each UTC day, all of that day's seals fold into one Merkle root — the single hash below. Copy a root anywhere you like (a tweet, a repo, a chain memo) and you hold an independent timestamp: nothing can be back-inserted into a day you mirrored without changing its root.

Loading the roots feed…
Construction (mirrorable, verify it yourself): leaves are the day's seals (SHA-256 hex), deduped and sorted ascending; each parent is sha256 of the two child hex strings concatenated (UTF-8); an unpaired node is promoted unchanged; a single leaf is its own root. Every receipt from a completed day replays its membership proof up to the day root in your browser on its verify page.
What mirroring proves: once a third party holds a day's root, that day's set of calls is frozen — the platform can't rewrite it without the mismatch showing. Roots for days with zero seals are omitted, never invented, and today's root is never published early. Not investment advice.
