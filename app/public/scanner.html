Read-only checks of what you are about to trust. Each reads the chain itself, through two readers that must agree, and says what it read: a count, never a verdict.

Bridge: how many verifiers

A LayerZero app chooses who verifies its messages. When one verifier’s word is enough, whoever runs or fools that verifier decides what the app receives.

In April 2026 a bridge was drained when a single verifier, fed by poisoned RPCs, was the only one that mattered. How many must agree is one fact about an app, not a safety rating: it says nothing about who runs them or how.
