What this proves: the call’s decision facts (symbol, direction, entry, stop, target, confidence, time — for Arena paper trades: symbol, direction, entry, leverage, exits, open time) hash to the seal shown — if any had been edited after sealing, the hash your browser computes would not match, and any difference between the sealed payload and the live record is flagged in red. Outcomes attach to the same sealed record later; losers can’t be deleted without leaving a hole in the stream.
Daily seal roots (live): at the end of each UTC day, every seal minted that day is folded into one Merkle root, published on the daily roots page (raw feed: /api/roots). A receipt from a completed day shows its proof above — replayed in your browser. Anyone can mirror the roots feed (a repo, a tweet, a chain memo); whoever holds a day’s root holds an independent timestamp for every call in it.
On-chain anchoring (live): completed days can be anchored on Base — the day’s root rides as calldata in a zero-value transaction, and the block timestamp bounds every seal in the day with a fact no one here controls. An anchored receipt shows the transaction above; an unanchored day says so plainly, and mirroring still covers it. Not investment advice.
