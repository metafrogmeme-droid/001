A soulbound sigil for the people who showed up. Minted free from your own wallet — gas only, one per wallet, the art generated and stored fully on-chain on Base. It cannot be bought from anyone and cannot be sold to anyone; the only way to hold one is to have been here.

What it is — and is not

🔗Soulbound. It can never be sold or transferred — ERC-5192 locked, forever. Every transfer path in the contract reverts.
🕯️Free. Minting costs network gas only. Nothing in the contract is payable — there is no price to raise later.
☝️One per wallet. The contract enforces it — not a promise, an arithmetic fact.
⛓️Fully on-chain art. Your rune is generated from its own mint and drawn by the contract itself — no server, no IPFS, nothing that can rot or be swapped.
🪨A badge, not an investment. It promises nothing, earns nothing, and can never be resold. Its only meaning is that you were here.

How to bear one

1Create a free account and open the dashboard.
2Link your wallet address — a signature proves it's yours; no key ever leaves your wallet.
3When the forge is live, mint from your own wallet. The server never signs transactions, never sends them, never holds keys — it only writes you the invitation.
