⚔️ Daily Duel

A card a day in five categories: Direction — call a coin LONG, SHORT or PASS with the Claw’s own call hidden until you commit; Back or fade — the Claw’s second call is shown, agree with it or go against it; Range — does a coin break a band either way; Bigger mover — which of two coins moves more; Football — who wins the day’s match, settled on the final score. The market settles each market call 24 hours after you make it. Beat the Claw and the round is worth double. Free, with nothing to lose but the argument.
