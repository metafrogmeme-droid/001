Claw Firewall

A testnet gate for one shared module. It caps outflows, delays a new recipient or a new approval, and checks Permit2 and USDC v2.2 signatures through ERC-1271. Your wallet sends every transaction. This page does not.

  • An approval that is already open is not stopped by the hook. Revoke it, or leave it in place on purpose. Revoke stays available, including while the account is frozen.
  • DAI, OpenZeppelin ERC20Permit and USDS call ecrecover before ERC-1271. This module does not gate those permits.
  • A facilitator that only accepts a plain v, r, s signature fails closed. isValidSignature does not recover one.
  • Someone who holds the 7702 root key bypasses the module with a direct transfer or by pointing the delegation at another contract or at the zero address.
  • Avalanche Fuji is an ERC-7579 smart account. It has no EIP-7702. The 7702 path is a test of whether the wallet allows delegation, on Base Sepolia, and only while the flag is on.
  • Installing the gate makes outstanding unused Permit2 and USDC v2.2 signatures fail until they are registered.
  • An unread chain is not a clean result. The scan is a best effort. The contract stores the hash you supply. It cannot prove the scan saw every approval.
  • A new recipient or a new approval waits 24 hours. Loosening a policy, adding an executor, or changing the vault waits 7 days. A freeze lasts at most 72 hours, and another freeze waits 7 days. The longest exit is about 11 days.
  • The guardian is a second device or a person you choose. It is never RUNECLAW. There is no RUNECLAW key in the contract. A rescue sends only to the vault you registered at install.
  • The rules on the account keep working if this site is offline. One shared module serves every account. A second account is an init, not a second deploy.
  • We never ask for a seed phrase or a private key. We never DM you first. This page is only at https://www.humanoid-traders.com/claw-firewall.

Leave a row checked only when you mean to keep that approval. A checked row is left in place. That is not a judgement.
