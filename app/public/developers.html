Guardian — safety checks for YOUR agent

Every tool below answers “what has RUNECLAW done?”. Four of them answer “is what your agent is about to do safe?” — the same models the Guardian pages run, on input you supply. scan_transaction flags prompt-injection, seed-phrase lures, drain and unlimited-approval language, phishing URLs and address poisoning before anything is signed. compile_intent turns a plain-language mandate into typed, revocable rules that name who enforces each one. stress_portfolio runs a hypothetical book through a −30% drop, an alt crash, a depeg, a cascade and a black swan. plan_escape sequences a dependency-aware unwind. They read no account, store nothing you send, and every answer is a heuristic with reasons — never a verdict, and never a signature.

The verification ethos

Don't trust the dashboard — verify the fills. The Proof-of-PnL bundle canonicalizes as JSON with recursively sorted keys, no whitespace, UTF-8 (every number is already a string); publish_hash = SHA-256(canonical). Your agent can re-derive it in any language and reject anything that doesn't match. Safety reads are heuristic flags with reasons, never verdicts. Past performance never predicts future results.

Trade-capable MCP tools are a separate, operator-gated decision and are not exposed here.
